Introduction

Advancing Marine Mammal Welfare

A comprehensive guide to modern preventative healthcare strategies

Healthy harbour seals don't stay healthy by chance. Behind every thriving animal in a zoo, aquarium or rehabilitation centre is a carefully planned system of preventative healthcare that integrates nutrition, enclosure design, environmental management and veterinary medicine.

Rather than focusing on treating illness after it occurs, modern captive management aims to identify and reduce health risks before they develop, improving welfare while supporting conservation goals (Gili, Meijer and Lacave, 2018).

This resource explores the evidence-based strategies used to protect the health of captive harbour seals (Phoca vitulina), from disease prevention and biosecurity to enclosure design and the latest welfare research. Whether you are a student, animal-care professional or wildlife enthusiast, these pages provide an accessible overview of current best practices grounded in scientific literature and contemporary zoological management (Mellor and Beausoleil, 2015).
